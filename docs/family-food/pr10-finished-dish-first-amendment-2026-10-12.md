## 1. FACT / ASSUMPTION / DECISION / OPEN QUESTION

**FACT (existing product intent):** the original MVP specification explicitly models `Recipe → MealBatch → Serving → HouseholdMember`, supports `freeze_stage=cooked`, and calls for a Prep screen showing what to cook, how much to freeze and when to use it. The Operating Manual treats batch cooking, freezing, defrosting and reduced weekday cooking as core. The currently accepted [Master Roadmap](master-roadmap.md) places PR10 → PR10-PDF → Gate 2 → consumer PWA; its Gate 2 is a **backend planning/PDF** vertical slice, not proof of actual household cooking.

**FACT (current constraints):** [PR10-ARCH](pr10-prep-freezer-implementation-contract.md) requires at least one safely executable, source-backed shared/advance operation spanning two MealEvents with **net physical-action count B<A** before any PR10 release. The accepted 30-candidate metadata [audit](pr10-prep-metadata-readiness-audit.md) had zero published batch/freezer/hold fields. PR10 shared-onion/external-pair studies [#217](pr10-s1-pair-review.md) and [#219](pr10-ext-pair-source-review.md) were **NO-GO** within their limited scopes. None demonstrates the absence of usable cooked-dish batches in the entire catalogue.

**ASSUMPTION to test:** one **fully cooked, source/quality-reviewed RecipeVersion**, executed as one batch for two distinct MealEvents and with a reviewed safe storage and subsequent-use path, can reduce *separate cooking sessions* and simplify family use. It does **not** automatically prove fewer total kitchen actions, elapsed minutes, lower cost, a measured quality improvement, or that any particular dish is freezer suitable.

**OWNER-APPROVED PRODUCT DIRECTION:** finished-dish batch cooking and verified frozen/refrigerated reuse are the **primary Prep product job**. Shared chopping of ingredients, pre-cooking raw intermediates, composite raw mince holding and cross-recipe component matching become a **separate later optimization**. The historic NO-GO receipts stand unchanged.

**PROPOSED CONTRACT DECISION:** amend PR10/PR10-META and Gate 2 explicitly to prove a truthful **finished-dish batch plan for >=2 actual MealEvents** with safe chilled/frozen transition, not generic cross-recipe raw-stage matching or mandatory net physical-action `B<A` at the initial *backend planning* gate. The original `B<A` metric becomes a separate, explicitly gated **work-saving claim/optimization acceptance**, and cannot be asserted by the base engine. This is a consequential acceptance change, effective **only after independent review and merge**.

**OPEN:** evidence-backed finished-dish pilot candidate and reviewed applicable food-safety policy, original source/rights, **time-authority admissibility for planned preparation/use intervals** (see §4.1), approved new metadata read path (D3), exact Gate 2 fixture pins (D2; see §3.1 research-only preflight), and the confirmed batch/inventory transaction model. No accepted runtime policy exists merely because this document names one.

## 2. Product boundary and owner responsibilities

Primary user-visible capability: “Приготовить раз для нескольких приёмов → разложить → сохранить → достать → безопасно разогреть → съесть”.

| Capability / status | Owning context | What is authoritative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source recipe and nutrition | `RecipeVersion` + Nutrition/Composition | Immutable recipe/ingredient forms, source data and nutrition; source-reviewed cooking and servings metadata |
| Planned demand | `MealPlan` / `HouseholdMealEvent` / `Serving` | Exact member participation, local date, `portion_servings` as Decimal, pinned version/revision |
| Derived meal-batch proposal | **Prep** / `PrepPlan` / `PrepTask` | A proposed cooking session, batch ingredient totals, planned portion split and *conditional* cooked storage/use steps |
| Confirmed cooking and stored ready-food stock | **Future bounded execution/PreparedBatch inventory context** | **Only** explicit confirmed cooking/output, portion packaging, state, storage location and dated transactions prove physical supply |
| Use of ready food | **Future consumption/confirmed-supply operation** | Confirmed withdrawal/discard or consumption by household and event; never debit a plan-only promise |
| Planned shopping need | **Shopping** | Buy ingredients **once for a planned common cooked batch**, account for verified prepared supply **only** under an accepted authority/quantity contract, preserve snapshot/provenance |
| Consumer | Today / Week / Купить / Заготовки / Дома | Proposed vs ready vs used and unknown statuses expressed in Russian; confirmation with minimal user entry |

**Terminology:** `CookedDishBatchProposal`, `StoredPortion`, `ConfirmedPreparedBatch` are **conceptual names**, not accepted database entities or permission to create extra tables. A confirmed prepared-food record is **not** the legacy CosmeticWorkshopOS `ProductionBatch`; it does not silently become a generic PantryItem either.

**Hard separation:** `PrepPlan generation != cooking confirmation != freezer stock != meal consumption`. No generator may create `PreparedBatch`, `PantryMovement`, reserve supplies, mark a task done, infer that storage exists, or use planned stock as a `PREPARED` source in Planner.

## 3. Candidate identity and precision: cook once, serve multiple events

1. Start from one accepted, immutable Household-owned MealPlan revision. Select **>=2 different MealEvent IDs** whose source/participation/recipe pins have been checked. The simplest pilot uses the **same pinned, verified RecipeVersion ID** on both events; a different-recipe shared stage is not required. A future recipe version is **not** interchangeable with a historical pinned version. A same-name dish with different version is not equivalent.
2. Each event's required servings = exact sum of participating `Serving.portion_servings` in Decimal. Total planned recipe servings = exact sum across planned events. Derive structured ingredient demand using the current authoritative scaling/food-form contract; do not reconstruct quantities from RecipeStep prose, source package ratios or external dataset nutrition.
3. Group only the same verified cooked-dish output state, reviewed preparation/batch capacity, compatible ingredient/form/allergen exclusions and household schedule. Verify each participant's hard exclusions and serving needs; do not claim that one common batch safely satisfies differing contraindications or variants merely from recipe name equality.
4. One **physical cooking execution session** is proposed with **event-indexed contributions**, planned split/containers, intended location (fridge/freezer) and later use intervals. Differences between source yield and actual cooked yield are UNKNOWN until confirmation. Planned counts do not become exact mass without a source-backed yield, and per-portion mass need not be inferred from `Serving.portion_servings` alone.
5. A future `PREPARED` or `LEFTOVER` event in Planner is eligible only after a **confirmed supply** read with verified owner/version, remaining quantity, usable interval and valid nutrient/food-form authority. Until then mark planned supply and cooking demand separately; no phantom inventory.
6. Shopping must not double-count ingredient consumption and prepared-food obligations: a planned batch has one ingredient demand for its summed portions, not one full batch per planned future event. Confirmed remaining prepared portions are distinct supply, never automatically “added” to raw FoodIngredient Pantry. Any Shopping/Planner input or fingerprint change needs its own explicit, reviewed cross-context contract and negative tests. No silent change to PR9 snapshots.

**Important fixture/D2 conflict:** the accepted 2026-09 synthetic Gate 2-like week had 21 MealEvents with 21 distinct recipe codes. Existing approved **D2 permits even proposing a replacement fixture only after safe shared work and net `B<A` are demonstrated**. The new cooked-dish-first Gate 2 would otherwise be blocked by this earlier rule. **This amendment therefore explicitly PROPOSES a narrow D2-FD exception**: for a fully cooked same-RecipeVersion batch, an alternate fixture may be **proposed for separate review** only after an independently accepted safe storage/use and one-cook-session-for-two-events proof, **without requiring net physical-action `B<A` at that preliminary planning gate**. Original D2 `B<A` remains applicable to the legacy shared-intermediate/raw optimization path, and `B<A` claims remain separately gated. **Until this amendment is independently reviewed and merged, original D2 fully applies.** No fixture is replaced or approved here. Old MealPlans, ShoppingLists, nutrition and historical test fixtures remain immutable.

## 4. Finished-dish preservation and safety authority

A cooked-dish batch becomes an **executable early-cooking/freezing suggestion** only when its actual resulting food state and timing have accepted, versioned, reviewable authority. Required evidence, at minimum:

| Evidence field | Required decision |
| --- | --- |
| Source dish and final form | Original recipe/card/source/version rights, exact cooked vs semi-cooked state, recipe output and quality suitability after reheating |
| Cooking endpoint and portion basis | Reviewed recipe-specific cooking/heat endpoint, exact source quantities and yield where known; per-event Decimal Serving attribution |
| Batch capability | Applicability of source method to total batch size, equipment/temperature/time assumptions; scale-dependent constraints |
| Cooling / handling | Source/policy-backed safe cooling and any transport/packaging time, sanitary separation; actual-user confirmation where applicable |
| Storage | Fridge vs freezer, container and applicable **starting state**, jurisdiction/policy version, safe temperature/interval and explicit unknowns |
| Use later | Date and **explicit time-authority mode (§4.1)**, intended session window or worst-case date bounds, approved thaw-or-direct-reheat method, required endpoint, quality caveats and contraindications; unknown clock/cooling/temperature fails closed |
| Quantity and history | Planned vs confirmed batch/portion counts, consumed/discarded reservations only where confirmed, freshness/staleness pins, partial failure path |
| Copyright and language | Separate source rights/translation basis, reviewed Russian actionable instructions and safe quantity-neutral source step rendering |

**Fail closed:** `UNKNOWN`, **missing bounded time authority (not necessarily an exact clock hour when the entire conservative date interval is independently proven safe)**, unsafe/unsupported food form, contradictory policy, capacity uncertainty, ambiguous numeric RecipeStep or unverified cooling/hold ⇒ **no executable freezer/advance task, no safe-through date, no stock**. An INCOMPLETE reviewable PrepPlan may show a Russian unresolved obligation. “Not verified” must not become “not freezable”: explicit UNKNOWN is distinct from reviewed unsuitable.

### 4.1 Time authority and interval proof for FD-META / Gate 2 (review correction)

**FACT:** current `HouseholdMealEvent` in `backend/app/domain/meal_plans.py` has `local_date`, `position`, `role`, `source_kind` and recipe/source pins, **not a planned meal clock time**. `MealEvent.position` or a meal-role label (LUNCH/DINNER) does **not** imply an hour; `MealPlan.created_at` and source dates do **not** attest to cooking, cooling, actual freezing or actual use. Neither the Planner nor the UI may invent a 12:00/19:00 default or treat `local_date` as an exact safe-held interval.

For the **planning proof only**, FD-META must declare a source-owned, versioned **time-authority mode** before approving any advance/freezer task:

1. **`REVIEWED_TIME_WINDOWS` (conditional):** an explicitly approved Prep scheduling input/policy provides bounded, Household-local **planned preparation start/end, completed-cooking/cooling boundary, storage entry, retrieval and final use/reheat windows** with timezone, source identity, version and validation of ordering. These are **proposals**, not observed household events. This amendment accepts **no new database field or implicit inference**: before relying on such a mode in production, a separately reviewed implementation contract must name the input owner, API/storage/fingerprint path, trusted policy source, default/override rules and transaction/error semantics. A research receipt may freeze independently supplied synthetic windows **only as assumptions** for testing policy applicability, never as production evidence.
2. **`DATE_ONLY_CONSERVATIVE` (narrow, conditional):** if the independently accepted, dish/form-specific **jurisdiction-applicable safety policy expressly supports safe applicability for the complete uncertainty interval**, an event's existing `local_date` and a separately bounded proposed preparation date may be used to derive **worst-case limits**: earliest possible preparation/storage boundary at the start of its local calendar date and latest possible use at the end of its local calendar date, with full timezone/DST transition handling. Use the most adverse possible ordering/elapsed interval (including the entire uncertain day span), not nominal noon or a guessed eating time. **DATE_ONLY never proves how fast food actually cooled, achieved/maintained a required temperature, was packed or was transported**: those conditions need independent applicable source/policy authority and later actual confirmation. If such facts are required but unverified, date-only is insufficient and no task is executable.
3. **`TIME_UNRESOLVED`:** no bounded, source-reviewed time interval or no policy that covers **every** admissible interval; missing safe cooling/storage-entry/retrieval evidence; contradictory jurisdiction policy; non-provable boundary or ambiguous timezone ⇒ `UNKNOWN` / `INCOMPLETE`. Withhold executable advance-cooking/freezing/thawing and “safe through” dates. Do not fill gaps with UI defaults, suggested meal roles or assumed consumer behavior.

**Pass rule:** for **every required time-dependent transition** (cooking completed → cooling → storage entry → kept chilled/frozen → retrieval/thaw/reheat → consumption), the policy's actual supported conditions must hold for **the full bounded planned interval**, not just a convenient point estimate. Pin policy version, source, time-authority mode, planned bounds, Household IANA timezone and explanation in the FD-META receipt; changed plan date, version, policy or time bounds invalidates the proof and downstream Prep/PDF snapshot. When a policy needs measured household actions or temperatures, the **real inventory/execution phase** must collect confirmation, not treat planning as observation. If no safe fully cooked dish scenario can be established under these modes, **FD-META and Gate 2 stay BLOCKED**; obtain a reviewed explicit time-schedule contract before runtime. No universal durations, invented temperatures or new migrations are approved here.

**Adversarial tests:** same dates but different real use hours; preparation one day before versus on same day; late-evening use and earliest preparation time; timezone/DST day with non-24-hour elapsed duration; unknown start/cooling time or refrigerator/freezer conditions; policy only approves a shorter interval than the worst-case bound; schedule change invalidates prior authorization; a date-only proof that covers all elapsed bounds but not cooling/temperature obligations must still fail. Test a genuine fully supported conservative-policy case only if an actual reviewed policy proves it; otherwise record an honest negative evidence fixture.

**Research anchors, NOT FamilyFoodOS production policy (jurisdiction-sensitive):**
- [USDA FSIS: Leftovers and Food Safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) — safe cooking, rapid cooling, small shallow portions, thaw/reheat guidance.
- [FoodSafety.gov: Cold Food Storage Chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts) — category-level fridge/freezer guidance; freezer duration table focuses on **quality**, not proof of individual home process.
- [NCHFP: Freezing Soups and Stews](https://nchfp.uga.edu/blog/freezing-soups-and-stews-a-good-wintertime-activity) and [Thawing Prepared or Cooked Foods](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/freeze/freeze-general-information/thawing-and-preparing-foods-for-serving/) — finished-dish examples and reuse constraints.
- [UK Food Standards Agency: How to chill, freeze and defrost food safely](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-to-chill-freeze-and-defrost-food-safely/how-to-chill-freeze-and-defrost-food-safely) — independent safety context with potentially **different national rules**. Do not combine incompatible jurisdictional guidelines into a fabricated universally valid shelf-life.

Each candidate must choose and justify one applicable, versioned policy/source basis for its intended consumer jurisdiction, legal status and household context, with independent review of recipe applicability and exceptions. Third-party public web text is not automatically licensed for commercial publication/translation. **No new shelf life, reheating temperature, freezer suitability or duration is accepted or displayed by this PR.**
