## 3. PR10-CORE — minimum safe, useful technical contract

**Definition:** `PrepPlan` is an immutable Household-owned proposal derived only from one pinned MealPlan revision, eligible MealEvent/Serving joins, exact version-pinned RecipeVersion/RecipeStep identities and a **reviewed** step-eligibility decision. A task is a genuine **source-backed executable preparatory action**, not a guessed freezer schedule or generated cooking story.

**Permitted executable task (all must be true):**
1. `COOK_RECIPE` MealEvent pins an accepted verified RecipeVersion; source process/rights and Russian instructions are accepted for this context. Step hash and correct order match that exact version.
2. Every quantity for the MealEvent comes from **authoritative structured RecipeIngredients scaled by the sum of the actual member Servings using Decimal**, with source form/unit validity. Embedded numeric prose is processed according to **PR10-ARCH §4.1 reviewed mapping**. Unreviewed numbers or English instructions are **withheld**, not interpolated by LLM.
3. The task is operationally actionable in the **same meal-preparation session/date** without requiring speculative cooling, earlier preparation, holding, storage, freezing, thaw, reheating, equipment-parallelism or batch-capacity claims. If the source requires missing safety/process authority even for immediate execution, it is **not executable**.
4. Its source/event/member contribution, canonical ingredients, explicit task order and step eligibility/version fingerprints remain traceable; repeated tasks are **not** merged merely by matching words or ingredients. `scheduled_local_date = required_meal_date` unless a separately accepted, safe time authority exists; this does **not** imply an executable clock time.
5. A preparatory task has a **specific kitchen action and object or ingredient/process** (e.g. source-reviewed cleaning/cutting/mixing or explicit preparatory step), not just "prepare dish", the recipe title or an instruction to inspect a recipe. Do not infer missing quantities/food forms from prose.

**Unresolved treatment:** preserve the accepted seven meal-source kinds. `ASSEMBLY`, `LEFTOVER`, `PREPARED` and `READY_MEAL` remain unresolved without their separately accepted source/supply authority; `EAT_OUT`/`ORDER_OUT` do not need home prep. `COOK_RECIPE` unknown/unsafe steps or missing authoritative classification become existing `PROCESS_STEPS_UNVERIFIED`, `STEP_QUANTITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `SAFE_BATCH_METADATA_MISSING` or `STORAGE_TRANSITION_UNVERIFIED` obligations **where applicable**, never an invented executable freezer task. Reject wholly corrupt RecipeVersion/Serving/Household/quantity joins without writing a plan. `COMPLETE` in the 3-table schema means mandatory **step coverage only**; it is **not** safe batch, freezer or consumer-value readiness. `INCOMPLETE` is a legitimate immutable positive partial plan.

**Status / snapshots:** source fingerprint includes MealPlan/RecipeVersion/step/classification versions and relevant policy/config; changed source makes derived Prep/PDF stale and requires explicit recomputation. Same exact authoritative input is idempotent; invalid sources fail before atomic publication. No `PrepPlan` generation step changes Shopping demand or Pantry inventory.

## 4. Revised PR10-CORE / Gate2 positive and negative proof (proposed)

**Before any PR10-CORE runtime**, one bounded docs-only **PR10-CORE implementation preflight** must inventory the **actual** accepted Gate2 fixture recipe versions and step hash/classification permissions, confirm the existing three-table schema and failure/transaction UoW rules still fit the narrower capability, and record a **GO/NO-GO** for *source-backed positive safe-now tasks*. Do **not** lift the batch FD-META gate by relabeling it READY; `PR10-FD-META=BLOCKED/TIME_UNRESOLVED` remains true for later batch capability.

**Gate2 must pass ALL of the following:**

- **Full real-service technical chain:** one accepted synthetic Household (three members, their accepted heterogeneous MealPatterns) over seven local-calendar days, **at least 30 verified eligible recipes** and **80–120 FoodIngredient** as already specified; `Household → Planner → complete MealPlan/individual Servings → Pantry-aware ShoppingList → PrepPlan → backend A4 PDF`, with `AI_ENABLED=false` and no Retail connector. No mocked Prep/Shopping authority or manual fake output substituting for the accepted path.
- **Positive Prep evidence:** the exact fixture must contain **at least two distinct `COOK_RECIPE` MealEvents** each with **at least one independently reviewed, nontrivial, Russian, source/version/step-hash-pinned preparatory task** passing §3's eligibility rules. Every positive task is paired to its exact scaled **structured** ingredient amounts, event and participating member `Serving`s. Its executable text is not unreviewed copied prose; same-day/local-date is established. No falsely asserted shared work; no `B<A`, saved minutes, safely storable batch or freezer proof is required for **this technical gate**.
- **Meaningful completion / negative coverage:** at least one valid event/step with missing process, storage, numeric or unsupported-source authority must be represented as **explicit unresolved** with the correct source/Household/step association, without executable safety claim. A deliberate negative **all-UNKNOWN/zero-actionable-recipe** fixture must **NOT PASS Gate2**, even if it can truthfully persist `INCOMPLETE` for diagnostic purposes. A legitimately out-of-home-only week may have zero home tasks but is **not** an eligible **positive Gate2 proof**. Missing required source pin or corrupt structured quantity must fail with **no partial persistence**.
- **Consistency / persistence:** immutable ordered task/unresolved lists, audited complete-vs-incomplete step coverage semantics, atomic UoW and conflict rollback, idempotent generation, versioned source staleness after MealPlan/Recipe/step policy changes, Household isolation, no Pantry/confirmed-stock mutation, stable semantic fingerprints and replay-safe historical versions. Source task eligibility must not leak across RecipeVersions.
- **PDF integration:** backend PDF contains plan/Serving, Shopping and **honest positive Prep task(s) plus unresolved warnings**, source revision/renderer pins and staleness; no invented freeze/duration/expiry instructions. The PDF cannot silently present `INCOMPLETE` as full ready-food value.
- **Reviewable receipt:** source pins, exact Russian instructions, member/Serving Decimal quantities, two qualifying event/task proofs, unresolved case and full end-to-end pass/negative trace are independently inspectable. A CI green check alone is not a substitute.

**Gate2 can remain blocked** if this positive minimum cannot be proven with verified current data; do not replace the household fixture, relax positive cases, write fictitious prep tasks or claim ready-for-family.

## 6. Mandatory PR10-BATCH/SAFE-REUSE product-value and release gate

**Position:** after technical Gate2; independent from Gate3 UX and Data Readiness/Shared-Deployment implementation. **MUST be accepted before enrolling any real households**, regardless of single-tenant vs shared deployment, and before claiming consumer Prep/Freezer value, beta or paid release. Passing Gate3 and Gate4 alone is **not enough**.

**Minimum positive proof** (not an invented numeric nutrition safety norm):

- One actual source/rights/RecipeVersion/step-hash-pinned **finished, cooked dish**, with independently reviewed applicability to the **entire prepared batch** and the specific storage form, verified produced/edible output and preservation quality; no raw-ingredient or cooked-form conflation.
- **Two distinct, accepted planned MealEvents** tied to the **same** reviewed cooked batch (or independently reviewed compatible source grouping), preserving all member-specific Servings, exclusions, precise total ingredient demand and Shopping consistency. A historical research-only MealPlan is not automatically an accepted release fixture; any replacement follows applicable D2/D2-FD and independent approval.
- Applicable independently approved consumer-jurisdiction **food-safety and time authority** for preparation → cooling → packaged freezer/fridge entry → storage → retrieval → thaw/reheat/use: actual scheduled/confirmed inputs and worst-case interval review, not guessed times. Missing timestamp, temperature, handling or recipe-specific mapping yields `BLOCKED/UNKNOWN`, never usable freezer stock.
- Reviewed executable **Russian** instructions (including numeric-step reconciliation and batch equipment/volume limits) and versioned publication/reader authority under D3 for any new process/safety metadata. Unknown freezer quality, expiry or capacity is a blocker to the proposed batch claim.
- A reproducible complete proof of **one planned cooked batch serving two MealEvents** and compared **two separately required cooking sessions → one planned cooking session** under applicable storage/time policy. An assertion of strictly fewer **total physical actions `B<A` or minutes saved** requires a **separate measured work-count/time receipt** with packaging/cooling/storage/reheat overhead counted; it cannot be inferred from 2→1 cooking sessions. Existing D2 `B<A` condition remains for raw/shared-intermediate fixture replacement, not retroactively rewritten.
- **Execution boundary:** PrepPlan creates **no confirmed inventory**. Before any `PREPARED` event or freezer stock can be shown as available to a real household, a separate approved `PR10-FD-EXEC` source/stock/transaction/confirmation contract and runtime must prove actual cooked amounts, safe confirmation and consumption/waste/Household ledger behavior; a planned-only demo may not masquerade as ready food.
- End-to-end consumer negative proofs: unknown/unapproved storage => not offered; planned-only stock => unavailable; stale source/policy => blocked/re-review; partial execution => no phantom portions; wrong Household and duplicate confirmation => atomic rollback and nondisclosure; missing applicability => safe failure.

**Release decision:** `SAFE_BATCH_READY` is a **separate** reviewed status/receipt, not equivalent to `PrepPlan.COMPLETE`, technical `Gate2 PASS`, or `Gate3 PASS`. Actual release conditions remain **conjunctive**: technical Gate2 + consumer Gate3 + Data Readiness + shared Gate4 when applicable + **SAFE_BATCH_READY** + independently reviewed **`PORTION_RELEASE_READY=PASS` (including unsupported-member handling under §6.1)** + normal privacy/operational release checks. Neither recording a risk nor owner acknowledgement alone can produce `PASS`. Until all applicable gates pass, **real-family release is `NO-GO`**; no AI or retailer dependence is introduced.
