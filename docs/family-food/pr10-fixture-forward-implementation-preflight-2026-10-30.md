## 1. FACT / ASSUMPTION / PRELIMINARY DECISION / OPEN

**FACT — accepted previous negative experiments:** The real #254 QA operation ran two independent Python processes, each using a new temporary SQLite database, real `PlannerService.generate_authoritative` and repository-backed MealPlan readback. Both yielded 3 members / 21 MealEvents / 42 exact Servings and 51/51 matching current source/ordered RecipeStep content, but output UUID-independent semantic SHA digests **`4c1c574c26b5262edd64687cde292f16ecfc262111233bf60ad003cfbd47fb0f`** and **`a7948dcc11e36131b11cd4397436ba30b9257a5b344d928d6430023258d36f04`** were **different** (only one recipe/date slot matched across the two new runs). Therefore the *existing ordinary seed path* has not demonstrated forward cross-process semantic determinism, even with identical logical source content. The #254 success-status CI verified an **honest NO-GO**, not a reproduced fixture.

**FACT — existing code seam:** [FoodRecipeCatalogueService](../../backend/app/services/food_recipes.py) accepts a **zero-argument `id_factory`**, used to generate Recipe, RecipeVersion, RecipeIngredient and RecipeStep UUIDs. [Ordinary composition](../../backend/app/persistence/sqlalchemy_core/food_recipe_composition.py) exposes `create_food_recipe_catalogue_service(engine)` **without** a factory parameter; [DC4-A3 seed](../../backend/app/seed/dc4_a3_russian_step_corrections.py) transits many nested Recipe seed publishers and repeats that ordinary factory. [HouseholdService](../../backend/app/services/households.py) and [MealPlanService](../../backend/app/services/meal_plans.py) have separate identity owners; default composition does not make every relevant ID deterministic. [Planner source](../../backend/app/domain/planner.py) uses `recipe_version_id.hex` to break candidate ties and `member_id.hex` for member ordering. **Normal UUID differences can affect real week selection**; omitting UUIDs from the final normalized hash does not cure nondeterministic selection.

**FACT — bounded reference/preservation:** [PR #196 exact historical archive](evidence/pr196-original-synthetic-fixture-receipt.json) stays immutable at 110,511 raw bytes and SHA-256 `6c157ddead856ca2d109a698f221fc89472fda897c8f6473ceea0672afed04ec`; its old 21×42 week SHA-256 `731ef32e7137740ed5b46a6044a85d8b2f818a847fdc039842460d442df5aeb6` remains an **archival** comparator, **not** the forward acceptance digest.
